# Art completion captures

Actual Godot runtime captures for the 2026-10-03 art pass. The plans are
[the main plan](../2026-10-02_art-completion-plan.md) and
[the A2 wall supplement](../2026-10-02_art-completion-wall-plan.md).
Each folder records its staged scenario and limitations:

- [Condition chart](chart/README.md): three poses, four conditions, corner and inventory at
  1280×720 and 1920×1080; contact sheets collect the comparisons.
- [Workbench and tall furnishings](props/README.md): real built bench, native source comparison,
  and north/south body depth around each tall furnishing.
- [Camp Stove](stove/README.md): carried icon in the bag, inspect pane and quick strip, plus a
  dropped item. Later chart captures are the authority for final chart geometry.
- [Light colours](lights/CAPTIONS.md): fire, electric, green and red sources, overlap/occlusion
  and a same-view noon control.
- [A2 walls](walls/README.md): open/closed doors, depth, native layout and unseen roof coverage
  at both sizes. All-seen frames are explicitly diagnostic. Outdoor ground under transparent
  side bands is a known presentation follow-up, recorded in the roadmap.

The implementation and verification record is in
[docs/23](../../../docs/23-roadmap.md#the-record-by-system). Capture drivers were temporary and
removed after use; these images preserve visual evidence, not executable fixtures.
