# Ashgrove, the main area — what to look at, 2026-09-10

`main_area_528.png` is the region on the canonical seed 20260805, at 2 px a tile. The gate
(`npm run godot:m2:region` → `M2_REGION_OK`, eight lanes) holds the properties; a look is
arbitrated by eye, which is what this is for.

## What you are seeing

| | |
|---|---|
| **top-left** | `district.town_center` — dense, narrow streets, most of the buildings |
| **top-right** | `district.forest_edge` — grass blocks, the river running north-south, the lake |
| **bottom-left** | `district.industrial_park` — hardstanding, units and warehouses, no green |
| **bottom-right** | `district.residential_suburb` — grass discs with houses on them |

The **yellow ring** is the colony. Nothing put it there: every cell is generated colonyless and the
annex is ranked across all four, so on this seed it chose the town centre on street frontage alone.
That is the owner's "the colony can be established anywhere" at generation time. The *runtime* half
— a player choosing where to establish — is Task 8 and is named in docs/23 rather than built.

## What to look at

- **Do the four read as four different places?** That is the whole claim of a region over a bigger
  district. Each cell is a full 256 m district generated by the ordinary generator and blitted in.
- **The seams.** 16 tiles between districts, with the connecting roads carved through the openings
  the districts had already cut in their own walls. They are derived rather than drawn, so they cost
  no randomness and land identically on every seed for a given pair.
- **The seam is far short of what docs/24 argues for** (300–500 m), and that is a real loss, not an
  oversight: roadside wrecks and road blockages have nowhere to live, and the walk-before-you-drive
  progression weakens. It is shipped because the measurement says a 2×2 at that spacing would be
  ~912 tiles and run at 0.46× real time. The amendment is written into docs/24 with the numbers.

## Numbers

528 × 528, assembled in **3.9 s**. 307 buildings, 189 vehicles, **521 loot sites across all five
loot tables** — the first map in the project to carry every location at once. All seven
survivability clauses answered, none skipped, and the flood from the colony gates reaches every cell.

## Not yet true

**The game still boots a single district.** Only the gate boots a region. Flipping the boot is the
next piece, and it is the one that moves the balance harness — along with two failures that would
otherwise be silent: the director's night packets would place almost nothing on a region map, and
boot population scales off the map side, which the countryside inflates. Both are written down in
docs/23's what's left.
